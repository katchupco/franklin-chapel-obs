FRANKLIN CHAPEL STREAM TEST

OBS installation:
1. Open OBS Studio.
2. Choose Scene Collection > Import.
3. Select Franklin-Chapel-Stream-Test.json.
4. Choose Scene Collection > Franklin Chapel • Stream Test.

Hosted test-screen URL:
https://katchupco.github.io/franklin-chapel-obs/stream-test-package/01-stream-test.html

The collection is separate from the worship and Back 2 School collections.
Use it before a broadcast to check framing, motion, audio, bitrate, stream output, and recording.
